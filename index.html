FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and processes.

What services does Sharma & Associates offer? +
We offer a comprehensive suite of financial services including Tax Advisory & Compliance, GST Services, Audit & Assurance, Business Registration, Financial Consulting, International Taxation, and Corporate Compliance. Each service is tailored to meet the specific needs of our clients.
How can I file my Income Tax Return through your firm? +
Simply reach out to us via our contact form or call us directly. Our tax experts will collect the necessary documents, review your financial situation, identify all applicable deductions, and file your ITR accurately and on time.
Do you provide services for NRIs? +
Yes, we specialize in NRI taxation and offer services including tax filing for NRIs, FEMA compliance, investment advisory for Indian assets, Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) benefits, and repatriation of funds.
What is the process for registering a new company? +
We handle the entire company registration process — from selecting the right business structure (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) to obtaining DIN, DSC, name approval, and certificate of incorporation. The typical process takes 10-15 working days.
How much do your services cost? +
Our fees vary based on the scope and complexity of work. We offer competitive and transparent pricing with no hidden charges. Contact us for a free initial consultation and customized quote based on your specific requirements.
Can you handle GST registration and return filing? +
Absolutely. We provide end-to-end GST services including new registration, return filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9), input tax credit reconciliation, GST audit support, and advisory on GST compliance matters.
Do you offer virtual/remote consultations? +
Yes, we offer virtual consultations via video call, phone, or email. Many of our clients, especially NRIs and businesses outside Delhi, work with us entirely remotely. Our cloud-based systems ensure seamless document sharing and collaboration.
What makes Sharma & Associates different from other CA firms? +
Our differentiators include 20+ years of experience, a dedicated team of specialists for each service area, technology-driven approach for accuracy and efficiency, proactive compliance management, and a strong track record of client satisfaction across 15+ countries.
