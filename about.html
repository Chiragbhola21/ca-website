Who We Are

A Legacy of Trust & Excellence

Sharma & Associates was established in 2005 with a vision to redefine chartered accountancy services in India. What started as a single-partner practice has grown into a multi-disciplinary firm serving clients across 15+ countries.

Our team of 25+ qualified professionals brings together expertise in taxation, audit, corporate law, and financial advisory. We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients, acting as their trusted financial partners rather than just service providers.

We leverage cutting-edge technology and deep domain expertise to deliver accurate, timely, and insightful financial services that empower businesses to make informed decisions and achieve sustainable growth.
